# Phase 5: Divine Multiplication

> "It is more blessed to give than to receive." — Acts 20:35

## The Vision

One scaffold. Infinite climbers. Each standing taller than the last.

### The Mechanism
Every participant adds wisdom. The system grows exponentially. Every insight is available to every person instantly. Knowledge compounds like interest—but belongs to all depositors equally.

### The Math of Divine Multiplication
- 1 contributor × 1 insight = 1 insight accessible to all
- 10 contributors × 10 insights = 100 insights accessible to all
- 100 contributors × 10 insights = 1,000 insights accessible to all
- Growth follows divine proportion φ = 1.618...
